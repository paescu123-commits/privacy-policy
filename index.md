# Privacy Policy for MUSIC TOOLS: Cut, Combine MP3, Convert from MP4

**Effective date:** 30 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MUSIC TOOLS: Cut, Combine MP3, Convert from MP4 ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use the App on Android. The App is developed and operated by Pascal Huber, 5210 Windisch, Switzerland.

If you have any questions, contact us at paescu123@gmail.com.

## Summary

- Your audio and video files are processed only on your device. We never upload, store or see them.
- The App requires no account and does not ask for your name, email or contacts.
- The App shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects certain device data to serve and measure ads.
- We do not run our own servers and do not receive any personal data about you.

## Your files

The App lets you cut and combine MP3 files and convert the audio of MP4 files to MP3. All of this happens locally on your device.

- The App accesses only the files you select in Android's system file picker, and the output folder you choose.
- Files are never sent to us or to any server.
- Results are saved on your device, in the location you choose.
- The App keeps temporary working files, such as converted audio and waveform data, in its private storage on your device. They are not shared, and they are removed when you uninstall the App or clear its storage.

We have no access to the content, names or metadata of your files.

The App displays banner ads provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). To serve, personalize and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

- Your device's advertising ID
- Device information (such as model, operating system version, language and screen size)
- IP address and approximate location derived from it

- Diagnostic and performance data related to ads

On supported Android versions, Google's ads software may also use Android's Privacy Sandbox features for ad topics and ad measurement.

### Your choices

- **Consent:** Where required by law, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the App asks for your choice about ads and data use through Google's consent form. Where that choice applies, you can change it at any time in the App: tap the info icon (ⓘ), then "Ad privacy settings".
- **Advertising ID:** You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. The exact location depends on your device, usually Settings → Privacy → Ads.

For more information, see [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Permissions

The App uses the following permissions:

- **Internet and network state:** used only to load ads and the consent form. Your files are never sent over the network.
- **Advertising ID and ad services (AD_ID and Android's ad services permissions):** used by Google's ads software as described under "Advertising".
- **Notifications:** used only to show the progress of exports. You can refuse this permission and the App still works.
- **Foreground service and wake lock:** used so an export can finish while the App is in the background, and by Google's ads software for its own background tasks.

The App does not request permission to read all your media. You select files yourself through Android's system file picker.
